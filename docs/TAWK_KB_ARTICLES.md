## Article 4 — Is my listing visible on Google?

### English

- **Title:** Is my listing visible on Google?
- **Category:** Listings & SEO
- **Tags:** seo, google, search, indexable, schema, structured data, sitemap
- **Body (paste as HTML):**

```html
<h2>Yes — your listings are visible on Google from day one.</h2>

<p>AHO is built SEO-first. Every published listing and every agent profile is indexable by search engines. Here's what we emit automatically:</p>

<h3>For each listing</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>RealEstateListing</strong> structured data (Schema.org) — price, location, beds, baths, area, status</li>
  <li><strong>Image sitemap</strong> entries — Google Image Search can surface your property photos directly</li>
  <li><strong>Hreflang</strong> tags pointing at EN + ES variants</li>
  <li>Inclusion in <a href="https://advertisehomes.online/sitemap.xml">/sitemap.xml</a> (submitted to Google Search Console)</li>
</ul>

<h3>For each agent profile</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>RealEstateAgent</strong> structured data — name, photo, bio, location, languages, specialties</li>
  <li><strong>AggregateRating + Review</strong> — your buyer reviews surface as star ratings in Google results</li>
  <li><strong>FAQPage</strong> — your profile FAQs can appear as "People also ask" rich results</li>
  <li><strong>BreadcrumbList</strong> — clean breadcrumb trails in search results</li>
</ul>

<h3>How to rank well</h3>
<p>Fill out your profile thoroughly. Each profile field maps to a specific Schema.org property that Google reads. The 8-field checklist with detailed reasoning is at <a href="https://advertisehomes.online/en/profile-guide">/profile-guide</a>.</p>

<h3>Indexing timeline</h3>
<p>Google typically discovers new listings within 24–72 hours after they're published. To accelerate: from your dashboard, share the listing on Facebook (Pro Automation plan) — the backlink helps Google find the URL faster.</p>
```

## Article 5 — How do I cancel my subscription?

### English

- **Title:** How do I cancel my subscription?
- **Category:** Account & subscription
- **Tags:** cancel, billing, subscription, stripe, refund, downgrade
- **Body (paste as HTML):**

```html
<h2>Cancelling your AHO subscription</h2>

<h3>Self-service in 3 clicks</h3>
<ol>
  <li>Go to <a href="https://advertisehomes.online/en/dashboard/profile">Dashboard → Profile</a></li>
  <li>Scroll to the <strong>Billing</strong> section, click <em>Manage subscription</em></li>
  <li>This opens the Stripe Customer Portal — click <em>Cancel subscription</em></li>
</ol>

<h3>What happens next</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Service continues</strong> until the end of your current billing period (e.g. if you paid monthly on the 15th and cancel on the 20th, you keep access until the next 15th).</li>
  <li><strong>Listings stay live</strong> until the period ends. After that, they auto-pause — they don't get deleted.</li>
  <li><strong>No data is removed.</strong> Your account, profile, listings, photos, reviews, lead history — all preserved indefinitely.</li>
  <li><strong>You can resubscribe anytime</strong> — paused listings come back live immediately, and your settings are intact.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Refunds</h3>
<p>We don't pro-rate the unused portion of the current period (Stripe's default behaviour). For exceptional cases — billing error, accidental upgrade, technical issue blocking your use — contact <a href="mailto:info@advertisehomes.online">info@advertisehomes.online</a> and we'll review case by case.</p>

<h3>Downgrade instead?</h3>
<p>If you want to keep AHO but on a cheaper plan, use the same Manage subscription button → <em>Update plan</em>. Pro Automation → Plus → Agent are all one-click; the change applies at the next renewal.</p>
```
